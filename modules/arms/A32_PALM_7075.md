## Galvanic + fatigue (Materials REVIEW_R1 §2, R-027 #8)
- CF backplate on 7075 is a severe couple (carbon cathodic; 7075 −750 mV calomel). Glass-fibre isolation ply or film adhesive on the bond land, sealed / wet-assembled fasteners, anodize all 7075 except bond faces.
- R3 window corners: check under the 10 kg grip (arm_loads_r1); 7075 fatigue 160 MPa. Not yet FEA'd.
