## A42.2 Wrist fork vs palm clash (palm dd5f70f limits ±40/±25; P-026 needs ±60/±30): FIXED, no ROM amendment
`wrist_clash_r4.json`. Point-sampled sweep, pitch about y then yaw about x.
- **Current:** fork cheeks 30×6×26 at |y| 14–20, z −7…+19; palm top 15 mm below the pivot. Gap 0 even at ±40/±25. This matches the A32 finding.
- **Fix (sphere rule):**
  - Fork and cross parts all inside r 24.5 mm of the pivot. Cheeks become 20×6×20 pin bosses at |y| 14–20.
  - The **palm wrist plate drops 12 mm**: top at 27 mm below the pivot.
  - The palm lug lives inside the cross. It is not in the sampled set; CAD has to show that lug-to-cheek clearance.
  - Result: ≥ 2.5 mm by construction for **any** pitch/yaw, and a sampled minimum of 4.91 mm over P-026 ±60/±30.
- **Consequences:**
  - The hand is 12 mm longer.
  - Wrist static at 10 kg: 7.4 → 8.58 N·m, need 12.87 ≤ 14.7 contract, against the D70 repeated peak of 36 N·m. The T5 reach elbow/shoulder change is under 1.2 N·m (not re-run).
  - Change to `palm_7075.py` is the palm owner's (wrist-plate z −12 mm). **Arms did not edit `palm_7075.py`.** Ask filed to Industrial Design/palm stream.
- **D70 placement constraint:** no wrist drive housing may sit in the palm swept region (r ≥ 27 mm from the pivot, |y| ≤ 43). The D70 pitch and yaw units ride on the roll output **above the pivot (z ≥ +27)** with a short output link to the pins, or sit outboard at |y| ≥ 45. **Actuators and ID own the CAD.** The extra wrist-segment length is not yet known, and the output link mass/backlash is not booked.

## A43: hand close under the power cap: PASS
`hand_close_r4.py`, light ODE, 0.1 ms Euler.
- **Architecture (Electronics):** 2 × RP40, independent rails A/B, each ≤ 40 W with a firmware cap of 1.67 A at 24 V. Flexors split 3/3. The 80 W burst is therefore 2 × 40 W, and **no single rail exceeds 40 W**.
- **Cited data (D68 PDFs):**
  - DCX 22 S 24 V: R 3.69 Ω, Kt 18.4 mNm/A, I0 35.9 mA, J 5.55 g·cm², I_nom 0.869 A
  - GPX 22 HP **103:1** (v3's 111:1 is not in the cited configs): η 0.65
  - DCX 16 S 24 V: R 83.1 Ω, I_nom 0.144 A
- **[EST] inputs:**
  - tendon load: return spring 10 N plus contact over the last 6 mm ramping to the T4 30 N tip (217 N tendon)
  - sheath friction × 1.3
  - 4 W logic on rail A
  - small drives holding at I_nom during the close (worst case)
  - ideal rails, equal share, finger inertia neglected

**Motor-to-rail list:**
| rail A (≤ 40 W) | rail B (≤ 40 W) |
|---|---|
| F1 thumb flex, F2 index flex, F3 middle flex (DCX22) | F4 ring flex, F5 little flex, F6 index MCP flex (DCX22) |
| S1 thumb rotation, S2 thumb abduction (DCX16) | S3 index abduction, S4 ring/little abduction, S5 spare/wrist-side (DCX16) |
| board logic 4 W | — |

**Results (loaded six-finger close, 30 mm, ending at 30 N tip):**
| case | rail A close | rail B close | rail A peak | rail B peak | flexor peak current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| nominal | **0.644 s** | **0.640 s** | 40.4 W (1.68 A bus) | 40.5 W (1.69 A) | 1.71 / 1.77 A |
| η 0.50 | 0.683 | 0.671 | 40.4 | 40.5 | — |
| hot R × 1.5 | 0.655 | 0.651 | 40.3 | 40.3 | 1.40 / 1.45 A |
| friction × 1.6 | 0.669 | 0.660 | 40.4 | 40.5 | — |

- **T4 ≤ 1 s: PASS in all cases, at ≤ 40 W per rail.** The close is voltage (speed) limited, not power limited. The 80 W burst is never needed on one rail, and the R-038 interim per-motor cap (≤ 52 W ≤ 1 s / 5 s) is not approached: peak is about 6.5 W per flexor.
- The 40.4–40.5 W peaks are a 1 % Euler overshoot. The firmware 1.67 A cap enforces the limit.
- **Hold at 30 N tip, all six flexors, continuous:**
  - friction × 1.0: 0.741 A/flexor (0.85 × I_nom), rails A/B 13.5 / 11.2 W. OK.
  - friction × 1.3: 0.952 A (**1.10 × I_nom**), rails 17.5 / 15.2 W.
  - **Flag:** a continuous 30 N grip is above the DCX 22 continuous current if sheath friction is ≥ 1.2 [EST]. The grip needs a duty limit or a friction test. T4's "30 N continuous" is at risk until the friction is measured (G5).
