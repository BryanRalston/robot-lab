                                       Radial spherical plain bearings are designed to accommodate radial and
                                       combined radial and axial loads, and also misalignment. This specific
                                       design includes a steel/steel sliding contact surface combination. The
                                       bearings require maintenance and can be relubricated only from the side.

                                       • Designed for radial and combined radial and axial loads
                                       • Suitable for heavy static, alternating or impact loads
