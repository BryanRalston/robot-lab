# Grok Build brief: structure FEA re-check r2 (fixes from DECISIONS L36)
The box (about 1.2 GB free) can only run h ≥ 3–4 single-material parts. Use `modules/legs/fea_r8.py`, which wraps `grokbuild/fea_r5.py` with the modified geometry (args: part, h, param; PAD env for the clevis). The pass targets are yield SF ≥ 1.5 and Goodman ≥ 1.5, at the 2 mm-off or converged value, with fall SF ≥ 2 where the charter requires it. Report h-convergence (h 4, 2, 1.5, local 1.0) for every item.
1. **Clevis:** `PAD=16 python fea_r8.py clevis <h> 6`. Box h4: 3BW+screw 147 MPa. Confirm at h2 and h1.5 that the peak converges now that the corner is filleted. Also check the pad against knee-tongue clearance over full knee flexion.
2. **Clamp:** `python fea_r8.py clamp 1.5 34` (arm depth 34, flush with the ring). Box h3 gives −24% vs baseline. Confirm rev 0.52× Goodman ≥ 1.5 at h1.5.
3. **Femur tongue:** `python fea_r8.py tongue 2 1` (55 mm tangent neck). Box h4: press+fault 234 MPa. At h2, plus a frictional contact model of the 60 MPa fit (μ 0.15 without compound; the bonded limit with Loctite 648-class compound), report bore opening (sectors in tension, gap) under design, hot+design and fault. If it still opens under fault, size an anti-rotation/radial ø5 dowel or 70 MPa interference (hoop limit SF ≥ 1.5).
4. **Foot:** `python fea_r8.py foot 2 45` (doubler collar r 30.2–45 × 3 mm, chamfer 2.5). Bug to fix first: in `run_foot` the steel mask is `z > 18.15`, which tags the aluminium collar as steel. Restrict it to r < 30.2. Fall toe: report the stress 2 mm off the flange edge and at the collar OD. If it fails, try collar r 50 or 4 mm.
5. **Tubes:** dent model with a bonded internal doubler 0.5 mm × 100 mm (DP490 0.10 mm layer) at mid-span, femur ø50 and tibia ø45. Report SF in the tube and the doubler, and the peel/shear in the bond (DP490 values in BOND_SPEC.md).
6. **Hip ring:** retired. It is the obsolete ø80 CRBH geometry that L21 replaced, so don't re-run it.
Deliverables: `results/r8gb/*.json`, a convergence table per item, and pass/fail.
