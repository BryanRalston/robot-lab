# WBC walk retune on sim/v3 ddfcff8 (RL Controls, 2026-10-21)

## Context
- Ownership: RL Controls owns the WBC walk since the handoff.
- The old walk leaned on a −45° MJCF ankle stop that the hardware does not have.
- The r2 1.2 m/s plan itself commands the ankle to −1.014 / −1.024 rad, with 4.7% of frames past −0.916.

## Retune (no controller code change)
- **Plan pre-pass `plan_ankle_heel.py`:** stance dorsiflexion is clamped at −0.70 rad by heel rise (early toe-off). Pelvis z is raised by the toe penetration this causes. Output: `plans/walk_1p2_r2_heel070.npz`.
- **Run settings:** `WBC_GATE=1 WBC_GATE_RATE=0.3 WBC_GATE_TO=0.25 WBC_COP_M=0.013`, with `WBC_PLAN` pointing at that plan.
- **Ankle result:** the measured stance ankle minimum is **−0.877 rad**, inside −0.916, and the stop is never touched. The old setup reached −0.936.

## Best result
- **13.78 s at a 1.2 m/s command. Not 20 s: FAIL.**
- Measured speed is 1.02–1.13 m/s, below the 1.2 command.
- **Fall mode:** forward pitch-over at trunk pitch +0.16 rad with a vx spike to 1.6 m/s.
- **Sensitivity:** the result is chaotically sensitive to the gate and CoP parameters (±3 s from 1–2 mm changes). That points to a marginal limit cycle, not a tuning gap.
- **Next step, not done:** regenerate the plan with ankle bounds inside −0.916 instead of post-clamping it.
- The run defaults are unchanged because the result is not a pass.
