## (3) CRBH 7013 A shoulder_roll bearing + 7075 stand-off (R-119/R-120, Arms R13 1ee8d73)
**Change:** CRBH 6013 → CRBH 7013 A (70×100×13), roll housing OD108/hub 70, plus a 7075 stand-off carrying the pad tray (pad centre Z −20).

**Heat path:**
- shoulder_roll losses ≈ 2–3.5 W (R059).
- The larger housing (+≈ 15 % area) runs ≈ 1–2 K cooler than R059's 50.5 °C carry / 57.5 °C still-air figures.
- **New:** the 7075 stand-off conducts housing heat to the pad tray (aluminium, short), so the pad's DP490 core bond sits ≈ at housing temperature: **≈ 49 °C carry walking, ≈ 56 °C still air** (vs DP490 60 °C, 4 K margin).
- EPP face unchanged (≤ 48 °C walking, ≤ 55 °C still air).

**Preload drift:**
- CRBH A has integral one-piece rings, so the housing fit does not set preload.
- An inner-to-outer ring ΔT of ≤ 5 K (steel, dp ≈ 85 mm) changes preload by ≈ 11.7e-6 × 85 × 5 ≈ **5 µm**, within normal C1/C0 preload tolerance.
- The Al housing vs steel ring at +25 K: ≈ 28 µm diametral growth at OD100 (loosens a slip fit). Arms' bolted flange (8 × M4, PCD62 + dowels) carries the location, so this is acceptable. Do not rely on an interference fit for location.

**Grease:**
- Bearing ≈ 50–57 °C vs the IKO standard lithium grease (−20…+120 °C range [cat]). **> 60 K margin.**

**Ruling: PASS**, no thermal change required. The still-air pad-bond margin is 4 K. If ID/Arms want more margin, add a 1 mm G10/PEI washer under the stand-off flange (≈ +8 K/W, pad bond ≈ 50 °C still air, ≈ +1 g) or move the core bond to EA 9396 (80 °C).
