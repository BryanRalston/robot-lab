Physics simulation (MuJoCo WASM)

This is the design's MJCF model (humanoid.xml, 67.7 kg, 33 actuated body joints, torque limits from the joint table) running live in your browser with the official @mujoco/mujoco 3.14 WebAssembly build. Controller: joint-space PD holding the standing pose, with torques clipped to each actuator's limit. Hands are rigid bodies in this version.
