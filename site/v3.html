Robot Lab v3: hardening loop

Bryan asked for a design that would hold up in the real world. v3 replaces every estimated actuator part with a catalog part (Kollmorgen TBM2G, Harmonic Drive CSG, Rollvis RV, IKO CRBH, Everest XCR, RLS AksIM-2, maxon, Molicel), adds bolts, bearings, cable drums, fins and a tendon hand to the CAD, and re-checks thermal, life, fall, fatigue and FEA. The loop started with 13 failures; 63.8 kg after fixes.
