## Reconciliation
Budget items not modeled as solids in the CAD: harness/connectors 2.0 kg, covers/padding 1.5 kg, thermal loop 1.0 kg (= 4.5 kg, from design.py MASS_ITEMS) plus fasteners and driver boards. CAD solids 58.5 kg + 4.5 kg = 63.0 kg vs 64.3 kg budget (−2 %). Per-group differences are mostly bookkeeping (the budget puts ankle actuators in the shank and hip actuators in "upper"; the CAD assigns parts to the body they ride on) — the foot is heavier in CAD (+1.5 kg: F/T sensor, universal cross, actuator rod ends ride on the foot) which matters for swing-leg inertia.

v2 FEA (scikit-fem, quadratic P2 tetrahedra, TetGen mesh via meshpy, linear elastic 7075-T6 E=71.7 GPa ν=0.33)

Loads = worst cases from v2/analysis/checks.py: landing 3×BW (BW = 64.3 kg × 9.81 = 631 N) with the checks.py bending levers (femur 0.12 m, tibia 0.10 m, hip lateral 0.10 m). Walking fatigue = 1.3×BW (linear scaling of the same field). Allowables: yield 503 MPa, fatigue ~159 MPa (as in checks.py, typical, not a certified value).

Hand check (beam theory, checks.py): femur ~72 MPa, tibia ~75 MPa at the root — FEA agrees within ~10 %. All SF ≥ 2, so no redesign was needed for strength. FEA did find a real CAD defect: the tibia tube stopped 2 mm short of the ankle fork (solver had a floating body); fixed by extending the tube to the fork and widening the fork to 62 mm. Limits: peaks sit at sharp re-entrant corners and load-application edges (no fillets modeled) and are mesh-dependent; femur/tibia were modeled at their highest-moment end only (lug/fork + 150 mm tube, cut-face loads = exact resultant). Pin/bearing contact, bolts and welds/bonds not modeled.

v2 CAD: interference results

Model: model.py (CadQuery, parametric; ~161 parts). Check: clash.py (exact OCC boolean intersection, >1 mm³, intended mates whitelisted). Range sweep: sweep.py (5° steps).

Standing pose: 72 clashes in the first assembly → 0 after the fixes listed as # FIX comments in model.py (yaw/roll yoke rebuilt, pelvis enlarged to enclose the yaw actuators, knee tongue/lever merged, ankle top spherical bearings, torso openings, battery/compute repackaged, linear elbow → rotary, head/neck re-stacked, ankle fork narrowed, hip bracket ring resized).

Range extremes (first sweep): knee clash-free only 0..40° (need 0..120°) and hip roll only 0..15°. Fixes: femur axis 18 mm forward; knee clevis bridge moved in front of the pin; knee lever re-clocked to 30° behind straight down (r = 52 mm, effective arm 26 mm at full extension, ≥45 mm over 30–120° [EST]); tibia axis 14 mm forward. Hip adduction is checked with both hips rolled together (pelvis over the stance foot); single-leg adduction at the narrow 200 mm stance hits the other leg, which is a whole-body self-collision constraint for the controller.
